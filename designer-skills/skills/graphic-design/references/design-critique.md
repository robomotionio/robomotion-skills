# Design critique

Run this before delivering — on your own output and when reviewing someone
else's. Be specific and actionable; "make it pop" is not feedback.

## The rubric (score each, fix the weakest)

1. **Purpose** — does it serve the brief (right format, message, placement)? An
   image that ignores the goal fails even if it's pretty.
2. **Focal point** — is there exactly one clear entry point for the eye? Or does
   attention scatter?
3. **Hierarchy** — does the eye move in the intended order? Is the most important
   element clearly the most prominent?
4. **Composition & balance** — deliberate placement (thirds/centered), balanced
   visual weight, no accidental tangents or awkward crops.
5. **Color** — disciplined palette (roles, ~60-30-10), accent on the focal point,
   on-brand. Any unintended color doing too much?
6. **Contrast & legibility** — enough contrast for any text/UI (WCAG AA); reads at
   the size and distance it'll actually be seen.
7. **Space** — enough breathing room and margins; negative space reserved where
   text will go; nothing cramped against edges.
8. **Consistency** — within a set, do palette, rendering, and mood match? Across a
   brand, does it follow the system?
9. **Craft** — alignment, even spacing, no stray artifacts/garbled text, no
   unwanted borders or watermarks from the model.
10. **Distinctiveness** — does it look intentional and specific, or generic/
    stock/"obviously AI"? If generic, the prompt lacked a style + palette + focal
    direction.

## Reviewing AI output specifically

- Scan for **artifacts**: malformed hands/objects, garbled text, repeated/melted
  patterns, accidental borders or frames, watermark-like marks.
- Check **set coherence**: put the images side by side — do they read as a family?
  If one drifts, regenerate it via the reference-image workflow, not from scratch.
- Check the **crop/aspect** matches the target placement.

## Giving feedback

- Lead with what works, then the highest-impact fix.
- Be concrete: "drop the background detail and add negative space top-left for the
  headline; pull the accent teal onto the focal object only" — not "cleaner".
- Prioritize: one or two changes that move it most, not ten nitpicks.
- Tie every note back to the brief and this rubric.
